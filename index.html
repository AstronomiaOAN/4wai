Be part of
The IV Workshop for Astronomy Beyond the Common Senses for Accessibility and Inclusion (4WAI) continues a growing international effort to promote equity, accessibility, and inclusion in astronomy. Building on previous editions held in Colombia, online, and Uruguay, the workshop brings together astronomers, educators, disability specialists, students, and science communicators to share experiences and innovative practices. The 4WAI provides a collaborative space to discuss inclusive methodologies, assistive technologies, and strategies that broaden participation in astronomy education, research, and outreach. Aligned with the values of the International Astronomical Union, the workshop highlights astronomy as a powerful tool for social inclusion and scientific enrichment.

TOPICS
Exploring the Topics of Astronomy Beyond the Common Senses
The workshop addresses a broad range of topics focused on accessibility, inclusion, and innovation in astronomy education, research, and outreach. Core themes include inclusive and universal design methodologies for astronomy teaching, the use of assistive technologies and multisensory approaches (tactile models, sonification, audio description, and haptic tools), and strategies to make astronomical data and observations accessible to diverse audiences. Additional topics cover astronomy outreach for blind and visually impaired communities, inclusive STEM education, community-based and participatory projects, accessibility in planetariums and science centers, and the role of digital tools, artificial intelligence, and open-source software in expanding access to astronomical knowledge. The workshop also highlights case studies, best practices, and collaborative initiatives that promote equity, social inclusion, and broader participation in astronomy across Latin America and beyond.

Event Venue
The workshop will take place within the framework of the Latin American Regional IAU Meeting (LARIM 2026), one of the most important astronomy meetings in the region. While the scientific activities of LARIM 2026 will be hosted at the official conference venues in Cancún, México, the workshop itself will be held at the facilities of the Colegio de Bachilleres Plantel Cancún Uno, a local educational institution that has kindly provided its infrastructure to support this activity. The venue offers a spacious classroom accommodating approximately 50 participants seated at shared tables and is equipped with a computer, projector, and internet connection.. The location offers a comfortable and collaborative environment that will foster scientific exchange, interdisciplinary dialogue, and engagement between researchers, educators, and students attending LARIM 2026.

Submit your contribution
We invite researchers, educators, students, and science communicators to submit abstracts for the IV Workshop on Astronomy Beyond the Common Senses for Accessibility and Inclusion (4WAI). Contributions may focus on inclusive practices, assistive and multisensory technologies, accessible astronomy education, research, outreach, and community-based initiatives. Accepted contributions will be presented as oral talks or posters, fostering exchange and collaboration among participants.
