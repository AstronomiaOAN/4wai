Circular Announcement
4th Workshop for Astronomy Beyond the Common Senses for Accessibility and Inclusion (4WAI 2026) We are pleased to announce the 4th Workshop for Astronomy Beyond the Common Senses for Accessibility and Inclusion (4WAI 2026), which will take place on 30–31 November 2026 in Cancún, México, within the framework of the Latin American Regional IAU Meeting (LARIM 2026). The 4WAI workshop continues a growing international effort to promote equity, accessibility, and inclusion in astronomy. Building on previous editions held in Colombia, online, and Uruguay, this event brings together astronomers, educators, disability specialists, students, and science communicators to share experiences and innovative practices.

The workshop will provide a collaborative platform to discuss:
- Inclusive and universal design methodologies in astronomy education
- Assistive technologies and multisensory approaches (tactile models, sonification, audio description, haptics)
Accessibility in astronomical data, observations, and outreach
Inclusive STEM education and community-based initiatives
Accessibility in planetariums and science centers
The role of digital tools, artificial intelligence, and open-source software in broadening access to astronomy

Aligned with the values of the International Astronomical Union, 4WAI highlights astronomy as a powerful tool for social inclusion and scientific enrichment, particularly across Latin America and beyond. We warmly invite researchers, educators, students, and practitioners to participate and contribute to this interdisciplinary exchange.
